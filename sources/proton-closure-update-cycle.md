## 2. The physical scale carried by the proton

The ordinary proton Compton wavelength is fixed by the proton mass, Planck's constant and the speed of light:

$$\lambda_{C,p}=\frac{h}{m_pc}=1.32140985360(41)\times10^{-15}\ \mathrm m.$$

The corresponding period and frequency follow algebraically:

$$\begin{aligned}\tau_{C,p}&=\frac{\lambda_{C,p}}{c}=\frac{h}{m_pc^2},\\ f_{C,p}&=\frac{m_pc^2}{h}.\end{aligned}$$

The numerical reference uses the [2022 CODATA constants](https://physics.nist.gov/cuu/Constants/Table/allascii.txt): $h=6.62607015\times10^{-34}\ \mathrm{J\,s}$ and $c=299792458\ \mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$ are exact in SI; $m_p=1.67262192595(52)\times10^{-27}\ \mathrm{kg}$ carries measurement uncertainty.

$$\tau_{C,p}\approx4.40774882\times10^{-24}\ \mathrm s,\qquad f_{C,p}\approx2.26873182\times10^{23}\ \mathrm{s}^{-1}.$$

This mass-derived wavelength is distinct from a measured proton charge radius. The reduced wavelength is $\bar\lambda_{C,p}=\hbar/(m_pc)=\lambda_{C,p}/(2\pi)$. Using the reduced quantity in a formula written for the ordinary wavelength changes its numerical value by $2\pi$.

Established relation A characteristic period from rest energy

The Compton period is the inverse frequency associated with $E_p=m_pc^2$ through $E=hf$. Its existence as a mass-derived scale does not itself demonstrate a mechanically rotating proton or a directly observed universal clock.

## 3. What Haramein's calculation contributes

In [The Schwarzschild Proton](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.3527190) (2010), Nassim Haramein studies a semiclassical configuration of two contiguous model protons and reports a period near $5.55\times10^{-23}\ \mathrm s$, with a frequency near $1.806\times10^{22}\ \mathrm{Hz}$. This provides the historical starting point for the present candidate. These are published model results; their publication does not establish the model physically.

The reported period is numerically close to $4\pi\tau_{C,p}$. Source inspection also exposes a factor-of-two ambiguity. Equation (8) in the [available paper](https://hiup.org/wp-content/uploads/2013/05/AIP_CP_SProton_Haramein.pdf) prints $t=2\pi r_p/v$. With its stated $r_p\approx1.32\ \mathrm{fm}$ and $v\approx c$, direct substitution gives about $2.77\times10^{-23}\ \mathrm s$. Recovering the quoted $5.55\times10^{-23}\ \mathrm s$ requires twice that effective path radius.

Source discrepancy The doubled path must be specified

The present article retains the candidate $4\pi\tau_{C,p}$. It does so through its own stated closure postulate. The match with the paper's reported number cannot yet count as an independently verified derivation of that coefficient.

The model's gravitational mass and the conventional proton rest mass also have different roles. Here $m_p$ always means the measured rest mass entering the Compton relation. The Schwarzschild-mass assumption is not required to perform this arithmetic.

## 4. The closure law and the status of 4π

A complete sphere has solid angle $\Omega_{\mathrm{sphere}}=4\pi\ \mathrm{sr}$. To write a multiplier independent of angular units, use its dimensionless magnitude:

$$\widehat\Omega=\frac{\Omega_{\mathrm{sphere}}}{1\ \mathrm{sr}}=4\pi.$$

Closure postulate Angular completeness fixes the cycle multiplier

$$\tau_\Delta=\widehat\Omega\tau_{C,p}.$$

$$\boxed{\tau_\Delta=\frac{4\pi\lambda_{C,p}}{c}=\frac{4\pi h}{m_pc^2},\qquad f_\Delta=\frac{m_pc^2}{4\pi h}.}$$

The units are seconds: $h/(m_pc^2)$ is action divided by energy. Geometry determines the angular measure; the postulate assigns its relation to elapsed duration. An angular domain by itself contains no traversal speed or processing law.

There are two mathematical routes to the same expression. A path model with effective radius $r_\Delta=2\lambda_{C,p}$ and speed $c$ yields $2\pi r_\Delta/c$. The angular-closure model yields $\widehat\Omega\lambda_{C,p}/c$. Their equal values do not establish equal mechanisms. Nor does the $4\pi$ coefficient alone establish a spinor's rotational return: that interpretation would require the representation and its physical dynamics.

The decisive theoretical task is to recover the coefficient from a specified selection-and-closure mechanism. If that construction returns another coefficient, the constitutive law must be revised.

Persistent oscillations in $C(k)$ would be a necessary diagnostic in this formulation. A finite experiment estimates coherence lifetime, spectral width and phase stability rather than taking an infinite-time limit. The VE factorization does not select $m=3$; a temporal period must be recovered from dynamics.

## 14. Predictions, simulations and failure conditions

The constitutive law first predicts the dimensionless relation $f_\Delta h/(m_pc^2)=1/(4\pi)$. It becomes experimentally discriminating when a derived observable exposes the proposed closure process independently of ordinary mass-energy phase relations. Computing the number from the same input constants is a consistency check.

- 

### Recover the duration from dynamics

Construct generation, selection, realization and persistence with both directed feedback relations. Determine closure in internal simulation steps before assigning SI time. Derive the link between those steps and a measured proton process. If the model produces $4\pi$ only because that coefficient was inserted, the simulation has explored the postulate's consequences.

- 

### Test collective temporal order

Measure $C(k)$, spectral peaks, phase stability and coherence lifetime while varying interaction strengths, disturbances and system size. Compare interacting and decoupled networks, plus controls with memory feedback removed. The proposed ordered phase requires a stable regime rather than an isolated tuned trajectory.

- 

### Specify an independent observable

Derive a measurable spectral feature, correlation function or response whose magnitude and conditions depend on the closure law. Give its coupling to the apparatus and quantitative competing predictions before collecting data. Proton mass alone does not supply that detection model.

- 

### Test the biological projection

For a specified intervention, define a target variable such as microtubule lattice organization, a cellular oscillation or a molecular-state distribution. Fix comparison conditions, timing, measurement noise and analysis in advance. The network hypothesis must predict how the target changes and what evidence distinguishes its coupling from other mechanisms.

- 

### Validate the network's physical consistency

Check conservation, causal ordering and frame transformations under the chosen spacetime realization. If a preferred ordering is required, state its consequences. If a covariant closure is claimed, demonstrate it in the update law. A global notation alone supplies neither result.

Failure conditions Each claim can be revised at its own level

A different derived closure coefficient revises the interval law. Loss of robust collective order revises the time-crystalline claim. Failure of a structure-preserving 64-state map revises the proposed correspondence. An independently derived observable that disagrees with measurement revises its physical mechanism. These outcomes return information to the next imaginative cycle.
