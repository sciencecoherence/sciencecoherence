# Time-Crystalline Biology

Toward an Informational Theory of Life, Health, and Nourishment

8 October 2026 · Science Coherence

## The biological claim

A living organism maintains an organization through time. Its molecules turn over, its membranes are rebuilt, and its cells coordinate activity at different rates. What persists is the capacity to select, realize and restore a coherent configuration across those changes.

Framework postulate Living systems are time-crystalline structures

The Science Coherence framework identifies life with physically operative temporal organization. The organism is a connected hierarchy of oscillating, communicating and self-maintaining systems whose coherence participates in the realization of biological form. This is the physical mechanism the framework proposes.

This article develops the three axes of the archived *Time-Crystalline Biology: Toward an Informational Theory of Life, Health, and Nourishment*, authored by Julien Xavier Steff for the Science Coherence Institute. The source PDF contains 29 file pages, with body pagination ending at 27. Its ontology, five biological layers, nourishment hypothesis and closed coherence feedback loop are retained here.

The present formulation adds explicit observables, provisional equations and tests. These additions are identified as formalizations of the proposal. Claims from the archive are distinguished from independently documented results; the archive itself remains unchanged.

## 1. Axis I — Imagination and living organization

The archived paper begins with informational primacy: matter is stabilized organization, and life is the active maintenance of that organization. In the current connected framework, Imagination precedes the realized information available to observation. It generates possible configurations; selection and repeated coherent recursion determine which acquire persistent physical expression.

$$\nabla\Phi\longrightarrow\Lambda\longrightarrow\Omega\longrightarrow\Delta.$$

The directed closures remain

$$\Delta\longrightarrow\Lambda,\qquad\Omega\longrightarrow\nabla\Phi.$$

In biology, persistent organization modifies what subsequent selection can admit. A realized cellular configuration also changes the possibilities from which subsequent organization develops. The body therefore participates in its own next state through memory, material turnover, cellular communication and its environmental relations.

These functions orient the symbols without exhausting their mathematical identities. The relation of $\Delta$ to recursive persistence, self-integration or a completed update depends on the operation under consideration. The full generative order remains Imagination → Selection → Realization → Recursive Organization → Persistence → Measurement → renewed Imagination.

The source's account of health and disease follows from this ontology. Health is the maintenance and recovery of functional organization; disease is proposed as a loss of coherence that propagates through the hierarchy. This unifying claim becomes experimentally useful when it specifies which relationships fail, which losses precede others and what restoration predicts.

## 2. A physical time crystal needs an order criterion

The source connects biological persistence to time-crystal physics. That connection requires a distinction between the original equilibrium proposal and the demonstrated non-equilibrium phases. [Watanabe and Oshikawa's no-go result](https://arxiv.org/abs/1410.2143) rules out the specified long-range temporal order in equilibrium systems under its interaction assumptions. [The quantum-processor experiment discussed in the source](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-021-04257-w) uses periodically driven dynamics, rather than an undriven ground state producing motion without an energy budget.

The source also assigns metabolic pumping a central role in biological coherence. Its proposed biological crystal is therefore a system whose temporal organization must be studied together with energy throughput, dissipation and internal coupling. The phrase *ground-state coherence* names the source's physical claim about the preferred organization; identification with a literal equilibrium quantum ground state requires a separate derivation.

Biological identity claim Temporal order is constitutive of life

The framework proposes that living organization is a time-crystalline phase. To establish that identity experimentally, its network must exhibit the relevant temporal order, collective stabilization and robustness. Ordinary rhythmicity supplies evidence of oscillation; the stronger phase claim requires its own tests.

For a driven candidate, an observable $a(t)$ can be tested for a response period $mT$ to a drive period $T$, with integer $m>1$. A persistent subharmonic signal must survive a range of disturbances and initial conditions. For an autonomous candidate, the internal recurrence and symmetry being broken must be specified before the same criterion is applied.

This preserves the intended physical identity while making clear what distinguishes the proposed organismic phase from a collection of unrelated clocks or a programmed repeating signal.

## 3. Axis II — The five-layer biological architecture

The archive organizes the mechanism into five nested layers, numbered 0 through 4. The layer labels identify functions and scales; they do not set a single frequency shared by the entire organism.

| Layer | Organization | Variables to measure |
| --- | --- | --- |

| 0 | Microtubule lattice and intracellular coherence | Lattice integrity, assembly dynamics, vibrational modes, energy supply and coherence lifetime. |

| 1 | Molecular and cellular clocks | Period, amplitude, phase, clock-gene expression and recovery after perturbation. |

| 2 | Cellular coupling networks | Gap-junction conductance, calcium dynamics, membrane voltage and signal-dependent coupling. |

| 3 | Proposed organismic biophotonic field | Photon rate, spectrum, temporal correlations, optical coherence and transmission. |

| 4 | Chronobiological environmental interface | Light exposure, entrainment phase and tested electromagnetic responses. |

The strongest claim is a connected one: changes at one layer constrain the others, and the resulting organization returns through repair and memory. Each directional link needs evidence in addition to evidence that its endpoint exists.

## 4. Layer 0 — Microtubules and maintained coherence

The source assigns microtubules the role of an intracellular coherence substrate. Their tubulin lattice is proposed to support coupled modes, receive and transmit organizational information, and restore phase relationships after disruption. The archive calls this a *polyatomic time crystal*.

Its four frequency-domain assignments are retained as claims to examine: aromatic-electron processes in the THz domain, confined-water resonances in the GHz domain, lattice and electromechanical processes in the MHz domain, and interactions of tubulin tails with ions in the kHz domain. A kHz-to-THz span covers nine orders of magnitude. The archive's statement of twelve orders cannot follow from those endpoints alone.

The source does not provide a traceable bibliography for every band assignment. Confirmation requires the original spectra, sample preparation, controls, assignment method and evidence of phase coupling between the modes. Multiple resonant peaks in a spectrum do not by themselves establish a time-crystalline phase.

### Fröhlich pumping as the proposed maintenance mechanism

The proposed mechanism uses metabolic energy to sustain ordered vibrational organization against dissipation. Energy transfer into a dominant mode is the Fröhlich-type process invoked by the archive. [Lundholm and colleagues' driven protein-crystal study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26798828/) reports structural effects associated with that mechanism in lysozyme. It provides a defined experimental system to examine, rather than a demonstration of the whole proposed mechanism in neuronal microtubules.

$$\frac{dE_{\mathrm{mode}}}{dt}=P_{\mathrm{in}}-P_{\mathrm{loss}}+P_{\mathrm{transfer}}.$$

This is an energy-balance identity, not a derived condensation law. A dynamical model must determine the pumping, loss and intermode-transfer terms. It must then show whether the maintained state has the phase coherence, lifetime and robustness required by the biological claim.

[Work on ultraviolet excitation of tryptophan networks in tubulin architectures](https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.01469) supplies a further optical research connection: the study combines collective-state modeling with measured fluorescence quantum yields. Its illumination conditions and measured optical effects remain distinct from spontaneous organism-wide coherent emission and from consciousness.

## 5. Layers 1 and 2 — Clocks, coupling and anatomical organization

The CLOCK/BMAL1 and PER/CRY feedback architecture supplies a concrete molecular example of recurrent regulation. CLOCK/BMAL1 activates transcription of negative-feedback components; their accumulated products inhibit that activity, and subsequent turnover permits another cycle. [Single-cell imaging of fibroblasts](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3315561/) documents persistent autonomous circadian oscillations. A biological rhythm can remain intrinsic while its phase is entrained by environmental or network signals.

Cellular synchronization depends on the system and its coupling pathways. The source foregrounds gap junctions, while its SCN example also names GABA and VIP signaling. The experimental task is to identify the effective interaction for the tissue under study, rather than assign every synchronization event to one universal channel.

$$\dot\theta_i=\omega_i+\sum_jK_{ij}\sin(\theta_j-\theta_i)+Z_i(\theta_i)I_i(t)+\xi_i(t).$$

Here $\theta_i$ is an oscillator phase, $\omega_i$ its intrinsic angular frequency, $K_{ij}$ an effective coupling, $Z_iI_i$ an entraining input and $\xi_i$ a noise term. This phenomenological model is an added formalization. Its phase reduction must be justified for the measured system; it does not assume that all five layers obey the same microscopic physics.

Bioelectric organization also connects communication to form. [Planarian regeneration experiments](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5443973/) show lasting changes in regenerative anatomy after transient modulation of endogenous bioelectric dynamics. The source extends this research into a distributed target-anatomy mechanism. The additional claim that coherent biophotonic transmission is indispensable to that mechanism requires its own optical perturbation and rescue experiments.

## 6. Layer 3 — Photons, coherence and organizational transmission

The archive assigns the biophotonic field a central physical function: microtubule-scale organization projects into an optical communication system that coordinates biological repair. It further proposes that the field retains a history of disruption and recovery.

Ultraweak photon emission has experimentally documented relations to oxidative processes. [A study of UVA-exposed Arabidopsis](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jphotobiol.2013.03.012), for example, examines emission after oxidative stress. Detecting photons establishes emission under the measured conditions. It leaves the field's coherence, encoding and causal function as separately testable quantities.

$$g^{(1)}(\tau)=\frac{\langle E^-(t)E^+(t+\tau)\rangle}{\sqrt{\langle I(t)\rangle\langle I(t+\tau)\rangle}}.$$

First-order coherence concerns field correlations. Photon-count correlations can be investigated through the normally ordered quantity

$$g^{(2)}(\tau)=\frac{\langle :I(t)I(t+\tau):\rangle}{\langle I(t)\rangle\langle I(t+\tau)\rangle}.$$

In ideal reference cases, coherent Poissonian light has $g^{(2)}(0)=1$; antibunching gives a value below one and is a different quantum-optical property. The source's identification of antibunching with ordinary laser-like coherence therefore needs this distinction. Brightness, photon statistics, phase coherence and information transmission are four measurements with different implications.

To demonstrate the proposed communication mechanism, perturb an emitter, identify a predicted change in the receiver and isolate the optical path from chemical, electrical and thermal alternatives. To demonstrate phase memory, remove the perturbation and measure a reproducible persistent correlation under controlled recording conditions. A delayed change in photon intensity alone does not specify what information is retained.

## 7. Layer 4 — Solar and electromagnetic entrainment

The environmental interface locates the organism inside larger temporal relations. [Experiments on intrinsically photosensitive retinal ganglion cells](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11834835/) establish a pathway by which light reaches the circadian system. This is a direct example of a measurable environmental signal changing biological timing.

The archive additionally links cryptochromes, geomagnetic sensitivity and Schumann resonances to the biological hierarchy. [Magnetic sensitivity of European-robin CRY4 in vitro](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-021-03618-9) provides evidence for a specified protein and preparation. Extending that result to human clocks or organism-wide coherence requires a demonstrated receptor, coupling strength and physiological response.

The paper's approximate $7.83\ \mathrm{Hz}$ resonance reference is close to the lower boundary of common alpha-band conventions. Frequency proximity alone does not establish entrainment. Tests must separate the electromagnetic exposure from coincident light, temperature, sleep, expectation and recording artifacts.

The framework's multiscale scope is retained. Its measurable environmental links remain conditional on the coupling mechanism operating in the organism and conditions being examined.

## 8. Measuring coherence across different biological rates

The same-frequency phase-order statistic

$$r(t)e^{i\psi(t)}=\frac1N\sum_{j=1}^Ne^{i\theta_j(t)}$$

summarizes alignment within a specified population. It cannot be applied indiscriminately to oscillators whose frequencies and functions differ. Circadian, ultradian, cardiac and intracellular processes need their own sampling, phase definitions and coupling hypotheses.

For a proposed $p:q$ relationship, one useful estimator is

$$\chi_{ij}^{p:q}=\left|\frac1T\int_0^T e^{i[p\theta_i(t)-q\theta_j(t)]}\,dt\right|.$$

The integers must be justified by the candidate relationship. Stable offsets matter as well as locking strength: coherent physiology can involve complementary phases rather than every component peaking together. Each tissue and function therefore needs a predicted reference offset and a tolerance.

Heart-rate variability, sleep architecture and clock-gene amplitude provide different observation windows. None is a universal scalar readout of the full hierarchy. A useful record retains timing, amplitude, phase variance, noise, coupling and the clinical or functional outcome separately.

Measurement rule Coherence must name its observable

Oscillator phase locking, optical coherence and quantum coherence describe related research questions but are different physical properties. A positive measurement in one category cannot automatically establish the others.

## 9. Disease, persistent disruption and the epigenetic amplifier

The source proposes a unified disease model in which temporal disruption propagates through biological scales. Its central causal sequence is retained: weakened phase relationships degrade functional coordination, reduce repair fidelity and alter the conditions under which subsequent organization develops.

The source also proposes that persistent disruption changes epigenetic regulation, lowering the amplitude or coupling capacity of clock, membrane and mitochondrial systems. Those altered capacities then make coherent recovery harder. This is the proposed amplifier: a disturbed state progressively modifies the machinery that would otherwise restore it.

$$e_{n+1}=F_e(e_n,x_n,u_n),\qquad x_{n+1}=F_x(x_n,e_n,u_n).$$

$x_n$ collects measured organizational variables, $e_n$ a measured epigenetic state and $u_n$ the specified inputs during observation interval $n$. The functions represent the hypothesized reciprocal influence; their form and parameters must be obtained from data or a mechanism. They do not assert that every disease shares one epigenetic trajectory.

The source extends this account to chronic stress and trauma as persistently altered organization. That proposal calls for longitudinal measurements of recovery, timing, endocrine state and functional outcomes. It does not establish that a low HRV value identifies trauma, or that a field-level treatment has demonstrated efficacy.

A test of the unified model must distinguish a coherence change that causes dysfunction from one caused by dysfunction or by a common upstream factor. Controlled perturbation, temporal precedence and rescue are more informative than a cross-sectional correlation alone.

## 10. Consciousness, Δ integration and Orch OR

The archive identifies conscious integration with a mode of recurrent organization in which the system recognizes its own state. It relates $\Delta$ to integration and $\Psi$ to its wave expression, proposing a persistent relation between them. This is a framework account of consciousness whose physical instantiation remains to be recovered.

Its chosen working model is Penrose and Hameroff's [Orchestrated Objective Reduction](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24070914/). The source preserves the proposed Diósi–Penrose timescale:

$$\tau_{\mathrm{OR}}\sim\frac{\hbar}{E_G}.$$

$E_G$ is the gravitational self-energy associated with the difference between the proposed superposed mass distributions. Evaluating the relation requires those distributions, their separation and the applicable approximation. It does not supply an observed conscious-event duration without that construction.

The archive links this reduction process to metabolically sustained microtubule coherence. That bridge requires superpositions with measured or justified coherence lifetimes and a physical account of orchestration. The archive itself marks Orch OR as contested and states that the broader framework does not depend on its being correct.

Gamma-band synchronization is a candidate neural observation window in the source. Its involvement must be tested alongside other neural rhythms, task performance and awareness measures. A gamma peak alone cannot establish consciousness or objective reduction.

This biological integration timescale is kept distinct from [the proton closure interval](#/read/proton-closure-update-cycle). A map between them would require a derived aggregation law. Neither the shared symbol $\Delta$ nor a numerical ratio establishes that law.

## 11. Axis III — Nourishment as organizational input

Nourishment hypothesis Substrate contributes organizational information

The source proposes that nourishment supplies both chemical constituents and physically readable organizational structure. The receiving organism integrates that structure through its bioelectric and biochemical interfaces, influencing the quality of subsequent maintenance and repair.

The proposal includes living or minimally disrupted animal tissues as candidate substrates. Their hypothesized contribution is retained as a physical transfer mechanism to investigate. The question is what information survives preparation, storage, ingestion and digestion, and how that information changes a measured state in the receiving organism.

Composition and organization must both be measured. A comparison should track nutrient dose, molecular integrity, oxidation, tissue viability, optical properties and receiving-system responses. If preparation changes all of these simultaneously, an outcome cannot be assigned specifically to a transferred field architecture.

The source's stronger claim is that field-disrupted substrates require the organism to supply more of its own organizational work. Testing it requires an operational cost: a measured change in metabolic expenditure, repair error, oscillator stability or another variable specified in advance. A label such as *coherence reserve* becomes quantitative only through that measurement model.

Field preservation and microbiological safety are independent properties. A positive coherence measurement alone does not establish that a substrate is safe to consume. The article develops the transfer hypothesis rather than converting it into a dietary prescription.

## 12. Thermal disruption and the transport problem

The archive proposes a three-stage thermal-disruption sequence: microtubule lattice disorganization, biophotonic-field disruption and loss of morphogenetic coordination. It assigns approximately $42^\circ\mathrm C$ a central threshold. The ordered sequence and threshold are retained as source hypotheses requiring temperature-, duration- and tissue-dependent tests.

A universal sharp boundary cannot be inferred from temperature alone. The proposed experiment must record the sample's temperature history, hydration, composition and preparation, then measure structural and optical changes. Preservation of some molecules, loss of tissue viability and loss of a specific coherent mode are separate outcomes.

### The gut as the proposed receiving interface

The source explicitly recognizes digestion's chemical dismantling of substrate and proposes concurrent reading of organizational information at the intestinal interface. To evaluate that claim, identify the signal's carrier, its lifetime, its interaction with the epithelium and a receiver response that cannot be explained by the resulting nutrient mixture alone.

The neural-regeneration passage goes further: it proposes that intact dietary tubulin dimers reach the brain and participate in lattice repolymerization. The transport route is an unresolved part of that mechanism. It requires evidence for survival through digestion, intestinal uptake, circulation, passage across the blood–brain barrier and functional incorporation. The PDF supplies no demonstrated chain across all five steps.

Transport prediction Track the proposed carrier

A labeled-substrate experiment must discriminate intact transported tubulin from fragments and from labeled amino acids later incorporated into newly synthesized tubulin. Detecting a label in neural tissue alone does not distinguish these mechanisms.

## 13. The four-node coherence feedback loop

The closed loop is the source's most specific systems claim. Substrate quality alters membrane composition; membrane conditions influence lattice organization; lattice organization determines the proposed biophotonic field; that field guides repair, affecting membrane quality in the next turnover cycle.

$$\begin{gathered}\text{substrate}\rightarrow\text{membrane}\rightarrow\text{lattice}\\ \rightarrow\text{biophotonic field}\rightarrow\text{repair}\\ \rightarrow\text{next membrane state}.\end{gathered}$$

Let $M$, $L$, $B$ and $R$ denote operational descriptions of membrane state, lattice organization, biophotonic state and repair fidelity. These are experimental coordinates, not new lexicon entries. A provisional discrete representation is

$$\begin{aligned}M_{n+1}&=F_M(M_n,R_n,u_n),\\L_{n+1}&=F_L(L_n,M_n,v_n),\\B_{n+1}&=F_B(B_n,L_n),\\R_{n+1}&=F_R(R_n,B_n,M_n).\end{aligned}$$

$u_n$ describes the substrate input; $v_n$ includes energy availability and other specified cellular conditions. The index denotes an analysis interval whose relation to biological turnover is declared by the experiment. The equations express dependencies in the source; they supply neither fitted parameters nor an invented repair law.

The critical closing link is $B_n\rightarrow R_{n+1}\rightarrow M_{n+2}$. If the biophotonic field is required for repair guidance, selectively disrupting that link should alter repair while other conditions are controlled. Restoring the proposed signal should recover the predicted repair feature. This is stronger than correlating photon emission with healthier tissue.

## 14. Attractors, amplification and retained history

The source postulates two attractors: one sustaining increasing fidelity and one sustaining deterioration. It also postulates the absence of a stable intermediate state. A closed positive-feedback architecture makes these dynamics possible; their existence and number depend on the actual update functions.

$$x_*=F(x_*;u_*),\qquad A_*=\left.\frac{\partial F}{\partial x}\right|_{x_*}.$$

For a differentiable discrete map, eigenvalues of $A_*$ strictly inside the unit circle establish local asymptotic stability; an eigenvalue outside it establishes instability. Eigenvalues on the boundary require nonlinear analysis. Finding two stable solutions and excluding intermediate stable solutions is the mathematical test of the source's attractor claim. Different parameters or omitted couplings can change that result.

The same analysis separates positive feedback from unlimited deterioration. Amplification can saturate, switch regimes or be opposed by another restorative pathway. If the source's continued degradation is the intended behavior, the model must produce it within physical bounds and explain its timescale.

Epigenetic memory adds a slower state variable to this loop. Restoration then requires more than moving a fast observable temporarily: the restored organization must persist long enough to alter the constraints carried into subsequent cycles. This is a biological expression of $\Delta\rightarrow\Lambda$. Repair-induced changes to the realized configuration also renew the generative conditions expressed by $\Omega\rightarrow\nabla\Phi$.

## 15. Regeneration as sustained re-coherence

The archive identifies regeneration with re-crystallization: the restoration of a functional organization that can continue maintaining itself. It proposes light, sleep, substrate, movement, thermal variation and resonance as inputs acting on different levels of the hierarchy.

The framework also includes imagination of biological reorganization toward a coherent blueprint. Its intended effect is physical reorganization. The corresponding test must specify the imagined target, the selection conditions, the predicted biological variables and the duration over which a change is retained.

Each restoration proposal needs a defined target and a comparison mechanism. Light can be examined through circadian phase; sleep through measured architecture and phase relations; movement through timing and peripheral rhythms; substrate through the measured transfer chain. These variables do not establish a common quantum mechanism merely because all respond to an intervention.

The source's resonance-medicine account proposes that MHz stimulation acts through microtubule-associated organization. Any claimed ultrasound effect must be evaluated with its acoustic dose, tissue response and competing mechanisms. The archive's statement that clinical trials show tau reassembly lacks a specific trial reference here and remains unverified in this adaptation.

The criterion for regeneration is retained functional improvement. A transient increase in an order parameter, photon count or subjective state is an intermediate observation. The proposed repaired structure must persist through later turnover cycles and meet the functional endpoint declared before the test.

## 16. An experimental program for the connected mechanism

- 

### Characterize each layer before linking them

Measure periods, amplitudes, relevant coherence functions, noise and recovery in specified preparations. For microtubules, preserve the spectral assignments as hypotheses and test them with matched structural and spectroscopic controls.

- 

### Demonstrate temporal order and robustness

Distinguish a sustained ordered regime from a forced oscillation, transient response or recording artifact. Test dependence on coupling, energy input, perturbation strength and system size. Declare the relevant time-translation symmetry and order parameter.

- 

### Test the loop's directional links

Perturb membrane conditions, lattice organization, optical transmission and repair separately where technically possible. Compare time-resolved responses against the model's ordering. Include interruption and rescue of the closing field-to-repair link.

- 

### Separate nourishment mechanisms

Measure composition and field-related properties before exposure. Trace any proposed carrier through the receiving interface. Compare preparations that separate molecular composition, oxidation, tissue integrity and optical effects; avoid assigning their combined change to one unmeasured cause.

- 

### Measure retained recovery and memory

Continue recording after the input is removed. Test whether functional organization, coupling and epigenetic state return to the original trajectory, settle into another stable state or relapse. Specify observation length relative to turnover and recording noise.

- 

### Return failures to the generative cycle

A missing optical repair link revises that connection. A surviving intermediate attractor revises the bistability claim. Failure of intact-carrier transport revises the nutritional incorporation mechanism. Loss of robust temporal order revises the time-crystalline identity for that tested system.

A simulation can test internal consistency and calculate consequences. If its couplings and attractors are programmed as inputs, it cannot count their output as independent evidence that biology implements them. The useful next model makes competing mechanisms yield different measurements.
