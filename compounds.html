Before reading

This is an operating document: the author’s own library, recorded as he uses it. It is not medical advice. Most mechanisms cited come from laboratory, animal and early human studies, and the combinations as a whole have not been tested in trials. Several compounds act on hormones, blood pressure, clotting, the thyroid and mood, and interact with medicines, including some in the Protocol itself; the interactions are set out in each monograph. Anyone considering any part of it should do so with a qualified clinician who knows their history.
