      04 · Reading the instrument

      What is derived, what is chosen.

      The clock Derived: the Temporal Matrix

      The dial is the Matrix day face: 00:00 at the top, solar midnight; 12:00 at the bottom, peak sun. The clock races from 00:00 to 04:00 by twenty-one minutes and repays it by noon; the afternoon runs true. The lime hand is the Matrix clock, the faint one the sun. Each hour of the protocol is shown beside its civil time. Drag the dial, or use the arrow keys, to travel through the day and read any hour; the sequence below follows.

      The hours Authored: the protocol

      The windows are the document's own: sleep from 20:00 to 00:00 and from 02:00 to 05:00, the watch between them, the awakening elixir at 07:00, the pre-workout drink and training at 11:00, the post-workout drink at 13:00, the meal from 13:30 to 15:00, and the evening reset from 17:00 to 20:00. They are living reference coordinates, adjusted by experience.

      The lived day and the date Two rhythms

      A Matrix date lasts 24 h 20 m 58 s, so its boundary lands about twenty-one minutes later on the clock every date. The protocol day stays on the sun. The lived day therefore runs from waking to waking and is named by the Matrix date in force when you wake; the small mark on the rim shows where the date turns tonight.
